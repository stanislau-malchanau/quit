# Vape Tracker: Quit Vaping

**A tracker to quit vaping and smoking with a cozy game: one tap on a craving, 2 minutes of breathing with Haru the red panda, and your valley comes back to life. One subscription opens everything: no ads and nothing else to buy. It is for anyone quitting disposable or pod vapes, cigarettes, heated tobacco or nicotine pouches, all at once or step by step. It counts time without nicotine, money saved and health milestones, and a slip erases nothing. It works offline, needs no account, and runs on iPhone, iPhone Duo and Apple Watch.**

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6826943436)

## How it works

1. Choose what you are quitting (vapes, cigarettes, heated tobacco or pouches) and your quit date. Stop at once, or lower a daily limit step by step.
2. When a craving hits, tap "Craving a Puff". Haru pulls it out of the haze as a grumpy little cloud and breathes with you for 2 minutes until it calms down.
3. The cloud turns into warm rain: every craving you get through brings a plot of the valley back to life, and every nicotine-free day clears another.
4. In Statistics, see time without nicotine, money saved, health milestones with their sources (WHO, NHS, CDC) and when your cravings come.

## Who it is for

People quitting vaping or smoking who want help at the very moment a craving hits, not just a counter, and who would rather breathe with a calm, cute character than read charts. It is a self-help tool and does not replace a doctor.

## Questions and answers

**Does it help with vaping, not just cigarettes?** Yes: disposable and pod vapes, cigarettes, heated tobacco and nicotine pouches.

**What happens if I slip?** Log it honestly in two taps. Every plot you brought back stays; only the time to your health milestones starts again.

**Can I quit gradually?** Yes. Pick your quit date, and the app lowers your daily limit step by step until then.

**Does it work offline? Where is my data?** Breathing, the valley and your stats work without internet, and no account is needed. Your records stay on your device and in your own iCloud; we cannot see them.

**Which devices and languages?** iPhone, including iPhone Duo folded or open, and Apple Watch with complications; home and lock screen widgets, a Live Activity, Control Center and Siri. 14 languages.

**How much does it cost?** Free to download. Everything opens with one subscription: 3 months for $24.99 or 1 week for $14.99, with no free trial and no ads. If your Apple Account is eligible, the first week may be offered for $0.99. Family Sharing is included.
