# Vape Tracker: Quit Vaping Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-31

This policy explains how Vape Tracker: Quit Vaping (shown as "Quit Vaping" on your Home
Screen, the "App"), made by Stanislau Malchanau ("we", "us"), handles your information.
Short version: **we do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your quit journey
stays on your device and in your own iCloud account.**

## 1. Data the App stores

The App stores the following on your device: what you are quitting (the kind of product,
how much you used a day and its price), your quit date and earlier attempts, the slips you
record, the cravings you live through with the App (when, how strong, what was around and
how long you breathed), the uses you count on the gradual plan, your savings goals, how far
your valley has grown and your settings. Your Apple Watch keeps a copy of the same data to
show your time without nicotine and to help you through a craving.

Because these records are about your nicotine use, some laws treat them as health-related
information. They never leave your device and your iCloud account (section 2), except as
described in sections 5 and 8, which never include them. We do not run servers that
receive this data, and we have no access to it.

## 2. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the App syncs your data through Apple's CloudKit, in the
private database of your own iCloud account, so that your iPhone and Apple Watch show the
same journey. This data is stored and protected by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy
(https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We cannot see, read or export it. You can turn sync
off in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

## 3. Health information and Apple Health

The App does not read from or write to Apple Health. The recovery milestones it shows (for
example 20 minutes, 3 days, 2 weeks, 3 months) are general information from public health
sources such as the WHO, the NHS and the CDC; they are not a diagnosis or medical advice
(see the Terms of Use).

## 4. Headphone motion and spatial sound

With AirPods or other supported headphones, the App can use the movement of your head to
keep the valley's sounds in place as you turn. This motion data is processed on your device
in real time, is never stored and never leaves your device.

## 5. Notifications, Live Activity and widgets

Notifications are created on your device. Widgets and Apple Watch complications read your
data on your device.

The App can show a Live Activity for your first 72 hours on the Lock Screen and in the
Dynamic Island. iOS lets a Live Activity run for only a few hours, so to keep it going
while the App is asleep, the App sends our small relay server the push tokens Apple gives
it for Live Activities and the schedule: the times to start or update it and what it shows
at each of them (the time since your quit date and the next milestone). No product, amount,
money, craving, slip, text you entered, name, email, account or device identifier is sent.
At each moment of the schedule the server asks Apple's push service to start or update the
Live Activity, and it deletes what it holds as soon as the scheduled push is sent or the
Live Activity ends. The server runs on Cloudflare Workers; like any internet service,
Cloudflare sees the IP address of the request while delivering it, and we do not store it.
If you turn Live Activities off for the App in iOS Settings, nothing is sent.

## 6. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements are reported to Apple's Game Center
and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center identity.

## 7. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your
payment details. Apple tells the App whether your subscription is active and which plan you
chose, with its price; section 8 says what of it goes to our usage analytics.

## 8. Usage analytics and crash reports

To learn which features help and where the App gets in the way, the App sends anonymous
usage signals to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service of TelemetryDeck GmbH (Augsburg,
Germany) with servers in the EU. A signal names an action in the App: for example, that a
step of the first launch was finished, that a craving was breathed through, that the
subscription screen was shown or a subscription started, or that the App asked for a
rating. It comes with a timestamp rounded to the hour and general technical details: the
App and iOS versions, the device model, the language, region and time zone, and display and
accessibility settings such as dark mode or text size. When a subscription starts, its
signal also carries the plan, its price converted to US dollars, the currency and the
country of your App Store, so that we can see which plans people choose.

A signal never contains what you are quitting, how much you used or spent, your quit date,
your cravings, slips or their reasons, any text you enter, your name, email address, Apple
Account or location. Your installation is identified only by a random identifier that the
App salts and hashes before sending, so the signals cannot be traced back to you or
followed across apps. TelemetryDeck stores no IP addresses and uses no cookies. We look at
these signals only as statistics to improve the App; we do not sell them, share them with
advertisers or use them to track you. The App contains no advertising or tracking code and
does not track you across other apps or websites.

Separately, if you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings > Privacy
& Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous usage
statistics and crash reports. They do not identify you.

## 9. What the App does not use

The App does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or Apple
Health data, and it has no account, sign-in, chat or social feed.

## 10. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information
from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

## 11. Data retention and deletion

Data stays as long as you keep it. You can delete a slip or a savings goal in the App at
any time, or everything by deleting the App and, for synced data, in iOS Settings > [your
name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Quit Vaping. Because we hold no copy, there is
nothing further for us to delete. The relay server keeps a Live Activity's tokens and
schedule only until the scheduled push is sent or that Live Activity ends. Usage signals
are anonymous and cannot be matched to you, so there is no personal record of them to look
up or delete.

## 12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA in
California, APPI in Japan, PIPA in Korea or LGPD in Brazil), you may have the right to
access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its
processing. Since we do not hold your data, you can exercise these rights directly on your
device and in iCloud as described above. You can also contact us with any request or
question, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

## 13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes,
tell you in the App.
